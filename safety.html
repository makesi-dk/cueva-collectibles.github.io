COLLECTOR CARE

Handle the collection with intention.

Keep hardware, cards and figures clean, protected and appropriately displayed.

Keep dry

Store paper products and electronics away from moisture and direct exposure.

Control light

Limit prolonged direct sunlight on cards, packaging and printed materials.

Check condition

Inspect older hardware, cables and cases before putting them into long-term display.
